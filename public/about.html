About Jane

Jane is a small adaptive learning app for kids: placement over a skill graph of about 1,600 topics, spaced review, an island map, daily quests and a parent report.

Why "Jane"? The name is a nod to Jane, the AI companion and teacher to Ender in Orson Scott Card's Ender's Game series (she first appears in Speaker for the Dead). No affiliation with the author or publishers.
